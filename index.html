At a glance: Bubble Sky is an offline single-player arcade game. The current release does not require login, does not display ads, and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. All game progress is stored strictly on your device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how DAEMON STACK ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you install or play the mobile game Bubble Sky (Android Package ID: vn.daemonstack.bubblesky). By using Bubble Sky, you acknowledge the practices described on this page.

2. Information Collection and Use

Bubble Sky is designed with privacy first. We do not require user registration or sign-in, and we do not collect, transmit to external servers, store remotely, sell, or share any personally identifiable information or device data, including:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, or physical address);
  • Device or advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID, IMEI, MAC address);
  • Location data, usage telemetry, crash logs sent over the network, or contacts.

3. Local Device Storage & Data Deletion

To preserve your gameplay experience, Bubble Sky saves non-personal game progress strictly locally inside the app's private storage on your device (in the local save file soap_bubble_save.cfg). Locally saved data includes:

  • High score and maximum combo streak records;
  • Mission progress, unlocked bubble skins, and selected sky theme;
  • In-game item inventory counts;
  • Audio preferences (music/SFX toggles) and language selection (English or Vietnamese).

How to delete your data: Because all progress resides exclusively on your device and never leaves it, you can permanently erase all saved Bubble Sky data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Bubble Sky → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app from your device.

4. Permissions & Offline Operation

Bubble Sky operates completely offline with no internet connection required. The app does not request access to sensitive Android permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, precise or approximate location, phone state, or external media storage.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The current release of Bubble Sky contains no third-party advertising networks, no third-party analytics SDKs, and no behavioral tracking tools. Note that Google Play itself may independently collect standard platform distribution or store installation diagnostics under Google's own privacy terms when you download the app through Google Play Store.

If future updates to Bubble Sky introduce optional features or third-party services (such as advertising SDKs), we will update this Privacy Policy and our Google Play Data Safety disclosures before releasing that update.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal data from children or users of any age. Because Bubble Sky neither collects personal information nor communicates with external servers, it complies with applicable children's privacy regulations (including COPPA and GDPR-K).

7. Data Security

All game state data is protected within your Android device's standard application sandbox and is never transmitted across any network by Bubble Sky.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time—for example, when adding new game capabilities or updating compliance disclosures. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Effective / Last Updated date at the top.

Bubble Sky được thiết kế đặt quyền riêng tư lên hàng đầu. Trò chơi không yêu cầu tạo tài khoản và không thu thập, truyền tải lên máy chủ ngoài, lưu trữ từ xa, bán hoặc chia sẻ bất kỳ dữ liệu cá nhân hay mã định danh thiết bị nào, bao gồm:
